# User walkthrough

The journey of a user through HabitLoop, from sign-up to an accepted recommendation. Start the
app with the [setup guide](setup.md) first.

## 1. Sign up

Open the app and choose **Register**. Enter your name, email and password. As you type a work
email, the **Tenant** field fills in from the domain (for example `nagarro.com`); a public email
such as gmail gets a private Personal space instead. Choose **Create account**.

## 2. Join a community

On **Communities**, join one from **Discover** or create your own. Use the switcher in the top bar
to move between your communities and your Personal space. Each community has its own feed,
challenges and leaderboards.

## 3. Create a habit

Choose **New activity**, for example **Evening walk**, category **Fitness**, **Daily** at
**8:00 PM**, target **7** per week, and save it.

## 4. Log progress

On the activity page, log an occurrence as **Missed**, **Done** (partly) or **Completed** (the
full target). Each log appears under **Log history**.

## 5. Watch the dashboard update

Open **Dashboard**. Today's counts, the success rate, the trend chart, the calendar heatmap,
the streak and the milestone badges update on their own, without a page reload.

## 6. Take part in challenges

**Home** shows one feed across all your communities. Each post is a challenge and names the
community it belongs to; the chips above the feed filter it to one community. Like, comment on
or join any challenge.

Open a community to see its page and choose **Post a challenge** to create one there, either
**Public** or **Members only**. Logging the matching activity moves you up the challenge's
leaderboard.

## 7. Connect a wearable

1. In the phone app, open **Wearable** and choose **Pair wearable**. The watch app receives a
   signing key.
2. On the watch, choose **Start workout**, then **Walk**, and **End** when done. The status goes
   **Sending**, **Sent**, **Logged**.
3. The workout appears in the watch's **History**, the phone's **Wearable** tab and the website's
   **Wearables** page, with its time, duration, steps and heart rate. It is also added to the
   mapped activity's log.

A workout can be deleted from any of the three places; it is removed everywhere.

## 8. Get a recommendation

Sign in as the seeded user `dhruv.goel@nagarro.com`, who has 30 days of history. Open
**Recommendations** and choose **Refresh suggestions**. The card for **Morning run** suggests
**7:00 AM** instead of **8:00 PM**, with the reason and a confidence level.

## 9. Accept it

Choose **Accept suggestion**. The card shows the new schedule, and **Morning run** is now
scheduled for **7:00 AM**.
